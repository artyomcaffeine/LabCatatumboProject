*Last updated: 27 October 2026.*

## We don't collect personal data.

This guide, on the web and in the app for Android and iOS, has no accounts or sign-ups, uses no cookies, has no ads or visitor statistics, and sends none of your data to anyone.

### What is stored on your device

Only **the language you choose**, so the guide opens in that language next time, and **the last weather forecast** downloaded by the "Weather and location" page, so you can see it offline. They are stored on your phone or in your browser (local storage) and never leave it. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or this site's data in your browser.

### The app

- All texts, photos and fonts are inside the app. It works offline and does not connect to any server of ours.
- Only the "Weather and location" page uses the internet (see below).
- It does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts or photos. Your location, only if you use it (see below).
- Google Play and the App Store handle their own data (downloads, payments, crash reports) under their own privacy policies.

### The website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any server, GitHub may log technical data about each visit, such as the IP address, for security reasons. This is explained in the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### The weather forecast

When you open "Weather and location", your device asks [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) for the forecast for a fixed point on the lake, not for your location. Like any server, Open-Meteo receives your connection's IP address. We send it no other data.

### Your location

It is only requested if you tap "Show my location" on "Weather and location", and your phone or browser asks for your permission first. Your precise location is used, on your device, to show you on the map and work out the distance to the places of the trip. It updates while the page is open and stops being used when you leave. It is not stored and not sent to anyone, including us.

### The map

The map downloads its data from [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/), based on OpenStreetMap, for the area shown on screen. Like any server, OpenFreeMap receives your connection's IP address and, from the parts of the map your device requests, the area you are looking at. If the map is centred on you, that shows roughly where you are. Offline, a simplified map inside the guide is used, without requesting anything from the internet.

### Links to other sites

Photo credits, sources and tour operators link to other sites, which open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

### Changes

If the guide starts using personal data, we will update this page before it does and change the date above.
